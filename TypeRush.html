TypeRush Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

In short: TypeRush does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. The app works fully offline.

1. Information we collect

None. TypeRush does not ask for an account, name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

2. Data stored on your device

To work properly, the app saves the following only on your own device, using Android's local storage (SharedPreferences):

  • Your typing records (best WPM, best accuracy, number of tests, daily challenge streak).
  • Custom texts that you choose to save.

This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in your phone settings or by uninstalling the app.

3. Internet and permissions

TypeRush does not use the internet and does not request any Android permissions.

4. Ads, analytics and tracking

The app contains no advertisements, no analytics, no crash-reporting services and no tracking of any kind. No third-party services or SDKs receive any data from the app.

5. Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information, it is safe for users of all ages, including children.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at contact@manzamart.website.
