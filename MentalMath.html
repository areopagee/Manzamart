Privacy Policy

MentalMath · Last updated: October 25, 2026

In short: MentalMath works completely offline. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no way to send data to us or to anyone else.

2. Data stored on your device

To show your progress, the app saves the following information locally on your device only (using Android’s standard app storage):

  • Scores, personal bests, and daily challenge results
  • Correct and incorrect answer counts, accuracy, streaks, and average response time
  • A short history of recent practice sessions
  • Your preferences (difficulty, answer mode, and daily goal)

This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset Statistics, by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

3. Permissions

MentalMath requests no permissions. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files, or network.

4. Internet, ads, and analytics

The app does not connect to the internet. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting services, and no third-party tracking or SDKs that collect data.

5. Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information, it is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share any data, because we do not have access to any.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at: contact@manzamart.website
