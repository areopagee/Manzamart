UnitWise – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

UnitWise (“the app”) is an offline unit converter for Android, provided by [Your name or company name] (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: we do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data.

1. Information we collect

None. UnitWise does not collect personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, files, or usage data. The app has no user accounts.

2. Information stored on your device

To work as you expect, the app saves the following only in its private storage on your device:

  • Your settings (theme, decimal places, number format)
  • Your pinned categories
  • Your favorite conversions
  • Your recent conversions (the values and units you converted)

This data never leaves your device. You can clear favorites and recent conversions in Settings, or delete everything by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app.

3. Permissions and internet access

UnitWise does not request any Android permissions and does not connect to the internet. When you tap “Copy result”, the text is placed on your device’s clipboard at your request; the app does not read the clipboard.

4. Advertising, analytics and third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. We do not share information with any third party.

5. Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Accuracy disclaimer

Conversions use standard conversion factors and results are rounded for display. The app is provided “as is” for general informational use and should not be relied on alone for medical, engineering, scientific, financial, legal or other safety-critical decisions.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at this address. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
